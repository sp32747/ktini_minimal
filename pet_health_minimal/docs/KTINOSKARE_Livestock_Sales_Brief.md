# KTINOSKARE — Cattle & Buffalo Product and Pilot Brief

## Page 1 — See animal patterns. Add climate context.

Turn cow and buffalo sensor history into condition scores, alerts and visual
trends that support a more informed review. A working software prototype,
available for demonstration to dairy operators, farm managers and animal-health
teams.

### One animal. Six screening signals.

| Signal | What the prototype reviews |
|---|---|
| Low oxygen | SpO2 patterns and changes from baseline |
| Fever pattern | Body-temperature trends with sensor context |
| Resting heart rate | Elevated heart rate when movement is low |
| Reduced activity | Movement patterns across sensor windows |
| Heat stress | Temperature, humidity and animal response |
| Cold stress | Cold-weather context and animal response |

### From uploaded readings to a clearer review

1. **Upload:** sensor CSV with climate data.
2. **Select:** region, species and animal.
3. **Score:** six model scores and threshold alerts.
4. **Review:** trends, health score and CSV export.

Bring vital signs and local conditions into one view. Inspect changes over
time. Download prediction histories to support discussion with your
animal-health team.

Prototype trained on synthetic data. Scores support human review; they do not
diagnose illness, prescribe treatment or establish validated early detection.

## Page 2 — Start with your animals. Validate in your conditions.

A focused pilot can establish data readiness and practical usefulness before
rollout.

### Five demonstration climates

Rajasthan: hot and dry. Kerala: hot and humid. Punjab: warm plains.
Karnataka: mild plateau. Kashmir: cold winter.

These are illustrative seasonal environments, not proof of performance across
India's real farms. The demonstration uses 30 simulated animals (15 cows and
15 buffaloes), 129,600 synthetic readings, 72 hours per animal and six trained
classifiers. Training, validation and testing use separate animals.

### What you can demonstrate today

- Local browser dashboard and CSV upload.
- Animal-specific condition scores and alerts.
- Health-score trends and downloadable results.
- Separate cow and buffalo features.
- Temperature, humidity and wind context.
- Review of six distinct condition patterns.

### Proposed pilot

1. **Agree the scope:** select farm, animals, priority conditions and review criteria.
2. **Check the data:** confirm sensors, units, timestamps and one-minute exports.
3. **Evaluate together:** compare predictions with independently reviewed farm records.
4. **Plan deployment:** use findings to scope calibration, integrations and rollout.

**Bring a sample animal-data export to the demo.** Define the farm scope, data
availability and success criteria for a pilot discussion.

Current delivery is batch CSV analysis. Live-device ingestion, external
notifications and production integrations need further work. Clinical accuracy
and financial outcomes are unproven.
